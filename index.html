Duties Act 2000

Landholder Duty（重点）

防止通过买卖公司/信托股份间接取得土地而逃避印花税。

  • Landholder：持有维州土地 ≥ $1 million 的公司或单位信托
  • Relevant Acquisition：取得足够比例权益即触发
  • $1–2 million 有相位公式；公开实体有 10% 优惠税率
“Landholder Duty applies when someone acquires an interest in a company or unit trust that holds Victorian land worth $1 million or more.”

Land Tax Act 2005

PPR 豁免（重点）

  • 核心看是否真正作为主住宅使用
  • 搬出并出租后通常失去豁免
  • 仅有“打算回来”的意愿通常不够
“The PPR exemption depends on actual use as the person’s home, not just ownership.”

Payroll Tax Grouping 详解

Common Control（s72）

同一个人或同一组人对多个业务有 controlling interest（通常 >50% 投票权）。可申请排除。

Related Bodies Corporate（s70）

按 Corporations Act 为控股/子公司关系。不能申请排除。

对比s70s72
能否排除不能可以
严格程度更高相对灵活

口语参考回答

Landholder Duty

Landholder Duty stops people avoiding transfer duty by buying shares instead of the land. It applies when you acquire a sufficient interest in a company or unit trust holding Victorian land worth $1 million or more.

Common Control

If the same person has a controlling interest in two businesses — usually more than 50% voting power — they are grouped. Exclusion is possible only if genuinely independent.

PPR

It depends on actual use as the home. Moving out and renting it usually ends the exemption, unless temporary absence rules apply.
